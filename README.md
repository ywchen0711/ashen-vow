# 灰誓騎士 The Ashen Vow

An original 8-bit role-playing game in Chinese and English. Play it at https://ywchen0711.github.io/ashen-vow/

Hale, captain of the Ashguard of Varn, carries a cursed blade that drinks its bearer's life. Ordered to seize the four
Lenses that guard the land, he begins to doubt his King, and is sent away with a sealed box that will burn a village.
With Brin the lancer, Nyra the young caller, Miel the acolyte, Gowan the old sage, Tal the monk and Dorrin the engineer,
he sets out to undo what he has done before the red moon, the Ember, wakes.

- **Side-view active-time battles.** Every fighter has a time gauge; the game waits while you choose (or not, in Active mode). Spells take a moment to cast.
- **Every friend fights differently.** Ashedge costs Hale his own HP. Brin leaps out of reach and lands on a foe. Nyra calls spirits, Gowan tries to remember forgotten spells, Tal focuses his strength, Dorrin builds gadgets. After his trial, Hale can step in front of a friend with Cover.
- **Front and back rows.** Back-row friends take and deal half physical damage. Change rows in battle with Left, or guard with Right.
- A world map with random encounters and an airship, ten bosses, shops, inns, save points, Chinese and English.

Controls: arrows/WASD move, Z/Enter confirm, X back (hold X to run in the field, or to flee a battle), C/Tab menu, M music. Touch buttons appear on phones.
